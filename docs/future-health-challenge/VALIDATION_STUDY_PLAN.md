# Validation study plan — Abu Dhabi pilot

NeuLens is a research-stage screening-support platform. The following study
would be required before any validated-performance claim. This plan is
deliberately conservative and mirrors the challenge's "Validation and local
population performance" outcome.

## Cohort
- 300–500 community-dwelling adults aged 55+ in Abu Dhabi, stratified by
  language (Arabic, English), age band (60–69, 70–79, 80+), education band
  (0–8, 9–12, 13+ years) and sensory access.
- Consent for data collection, optional research sharing, and linkage to
  clinical records where authorized.

## Reference standard
- Consensus clinical diagnosis by a multidisciplinary panel (neurology,
  geriatrics, neuropsychology) using standard criteria, blinded to NeuLens
  outputs. Reversible causes investigated per protocol.

## Endpoints
- Completion rate (session-level, quality.completion = FULL).
- Test–retest reliability: ICC(2,1) for overall and domain scores between
  two comparable sessions 2–4 weeks apart under matched conditions.
- Sensitivity and specificity for cognitive impairment vs. reference
  standard, with 95% confidence intervals.
- Ability to identify meaningful change: reliable-change indices and
  ROC for decline between baseline and follow-up.
- False-referral burden: proportion of referrals not confirmed by the
  reference standard.
- Time to clinical assessment: median hours from assessment to clinician
  review and to specialist appointment.

## Segment analysis
- All endpoints reported by language, age band, education band, gender and
  sensory-access group. Small cells suppressed or reported with caveats.

## Acceptance criteria before any clinical claim
- All Arabic task stimuli translated (two items are administered in Arabic today;
  the rest remain English), independently back-translated, cognitively pretested
  with local participants, and signed off by local clinicians.
- ICC >= 0.70 for overall score; sensitivity/specificity with lower CI
  bound >= 0.70 in the target population.
- Reliable-change thresholds derived and pre-registered, not post-hoc.

## What the prototype already provides
- Per-language readiness registry and clinician sign-off workflow.
- Descriptive within-person stability (mean absolute change, SD) — not a
  reliability coefficient.
- Segment-level counts, comparability and review rates.
- Median hours to clinical review and referral outcomes.
- Explicit "not available" reporting where data is insufficient.
